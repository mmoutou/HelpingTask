# HelpingTask
Code and resources for Helping Task, Moutoussis - Kwan - Zavlis - Shin - Selai et al

Here we present two classes of models, to understand the Help-seeker - Helper interactions
using the 'Helping Task', developed by Taylor Kwan, Caroline Selai, Jae Shin, Orestis Zavlis,
and Caroline Selai, also in collaboration with Giles Story. 

The models provided here fall into two classes: Hidden-Markov models, in the 
tradition of Guennouni and Speekenbrink, which we term 'Hidden Markov Trust State' models, or
HMTS; And 'Emotion-Mentalizing Self-Other' models, in the tradition of 
'When Collaboration Falters, Insensitivity to How Our Actions Affect Others Drives Inflated Self-evaluations', 
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s42113-025-00250-y
based on the work of Andreas Hula, Peter Dayan, Karl Friston and especially Peter Fonagy.
